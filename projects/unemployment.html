The question

In spring 2020 unemployment jumped almost everywhere. How big was the jump in each part of New York, and how long did it take each county, metro area and region to get back to where it was before? Every figure below is computed in your browser from the processed data files, so none is typed in by hand.

      How to read this, and what it leaves out

      The unemployment rate is the share of the labor force (people working or actively looking for work) who are looking and have no job. It does not count people who stopped looking, so a falling rate can partly reflect people leaving the labor force. County figures are model-based estimates, and small counties are noisier than large ones.

    What the numbers say

    Find a park near you

    New York State groups its parks into regions. Pick the region you live in or plan to visit to see its facilities, busiest first. Regions are OPRHP's own groupings, not exact distances, so a park in a neighboring region may be closer to you. The chart shows how that region's visits have moved over the years, set against the other regions; the list of its parks follows.

    ParkCountyVisitsChange since 2003-05

    How the lists were built

    Attendance is a count of visits reported by each facility, not of distinct people, and methods of counting differ by site (for example, gate counts versus estimates). Facilities open, close or change how they count, so the system total partly grows because facilities were added.

Built, in the page's own script and style block (no new library, no shared file, no new data file: it uses the columns already in `data/crashes/county_frequency_severity.csv`, which the page already loads): (1) a "Rank counties by" menu with three choices: crashes per 1,000 residents (the view on load, unchanged), fatal crashes per 100,000 residents, and injury-or-fatal share of crashes; the bar chart shows the top 15 on the chosen measure and the all-counties table is re-sorted to match, with an arrow on the sorted column; (2) a dashed line on the chart at the median county, labelled with its value (26.0, 8.8 and 15.7%; the first and third are read from `analysis_meta.json`, the fatal-rate median is computed in the browser from the 62 county rows); (3) in the fatal-rate view, an asterisk on the chart label of any county with fewer than 20 fatal crashes in four years, as the table already had, and a count of them in the caption (4 of the top 15: Hamilton, Yates, Schuyler, Seneca); (4) a "Find a county" menu of the 62 counties: choosing one prints a sentence, announced to screen readers, with its population, crash and fatal-crash counts, its value and rank on all three measures and its group; draws its bar in gold (added as a 16th bar with its rank if it is outside the top 15); and highlights its table row and scrolls the table's box (not the page) to it.
